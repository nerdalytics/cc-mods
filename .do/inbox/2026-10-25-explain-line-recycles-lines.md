# The explanation line should reuse a line that is already there

**Status:** needs-triage

In cockpit 1.2.0 the explanation line keeps one blank line under the other lines whenever nothing is hovered, as the explanation-row spec of limit-pace-gauge requires ("Nothing to show: the explanation line draws as one blank line") after the user's Q6 ruling. In the user's live check on 2026-10-25 (screenshots in session de976edb), the status row was followed by the compaction offer "◆ You're out of the Smart zone. Same model, deep into the dumb zone now. [Compact] [X]" and then that empty line. Hovering the 7d tag filled the empty line with "7d limit: 78% used, resets in 3d23h. At this pace it runs out 3d3h before it resets." The user wants lines recycled instead: the hovered explanation should take over a line that is already drawn, such as the Smart zone offer, rather than keeping a line of its own free for it. This reading of the user's words "recycle lines" is the steering session's and is unconfirmed. Points to settle: which line a hover takes over when several are shown (the offers, the run-out warning) and when none is; whether the explanation line still needs its resting blank line once hovers reuse other lines, since the hover scopes only need somewhere to reveal the text; and whether the engine lets an absolute overlay cover a line above its own Box (an offset outside the Box's area), which neither the types nor a live terminal have been checked for. The earlier note `2026-10-25-explain-line-resting-metric.md` proposes filling the same blank line with a metric instead; the two ideas pull in different directions and want one decision.
