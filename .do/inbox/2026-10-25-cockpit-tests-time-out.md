# Cockpit tests time out at 5000 ms now and then

**Status:** needs-triage

Two time-driven tests in `cockpit/tests/cockpit.test.ts` failed once each with a 5000 ms timeout and passed on the next run of the same tree, during the limit-pace-gauge run on 2026-10-25. "asks gh 2 times in 12 s for the pull request in the status row" (the template at `cockpit/tests/cockpit.test.ts:2010`) timed out once in ticket 03's implementer run, by its own report. "draws 100 tok/s in the closing line for 700 output tokens over 7 s" (the template at `cockpit/tests/cockpit.test.ts:2066`) failed once in the main checkout after ticket 06 landed at 10ea4a6, `(fail) ... [5608.59ms]` with `Error: timed out after 5000 ms`, by the merger's report, and passed on its next plain run (205 pass, 0 fail). A third test, "draws a spacer among the lines as a blank line on its side of the prompt, spacers that meet as one", timed out at 5000 ms once in ticket 07's retry and passed in every later run, by the implementer's report. In ticket 08's implementer run, one run failed two more tests the same way, "measures a 1.5M window against the compaction threshold the breakdown reports" and a new classic paused-5h test, and the rerun passed 233 to 0, by its report; the new test took 40 ms in the final run, so the timeout looks like the suite's load rather than the test. The first two tests simulate seconds of time (12 s, 7 s) and neither touches the pace code. Unverified: whether they lean on wall-clock time rather than the test kit's simulated clock, and how often they fail; a loop of `claude plugin test cockpit` runs would give a rate. A flaky failure looks to `/do:apply` like a ticket's tests failing after its red-green work, which is the one case the user wants to be asked about.

Partial answer to the unverified point, from a scout on 2026-10-26: cockpit's pace and limit code reads the global `Date.now()`, not `$.clock` (`cockpit/hooks/cockpit.ts:929`, `1037`, `1114`, `1306`; `$.clock.now()` only at `1055`, `1085`, `1323`, `1361`, `1545`), and the tests build `resetsAt` from the real `Date.now()` (`cockpit/tests/cockpit.test.ts:139-141`, `225-234`) while `mock.clock` answers `$.clock` only (`cockpit/.claude-plugin/types/claude-code/index.d.ts:16159-16171`). Whether `mock.clock` also fakes the global `Date` is not documented there.
